# Week 14 Assignment: Polish and Present Your Portfolio

**Days 92–100 · Topics: Resume, GitHub Profile, Open Source, Interview Prep, Deployment**

Your final week is about presenting your skills to the world. These deliverables are real artefacts you will use in job applications.

## What to Build
- A polished GitHub profile README (`username/username` repo) featuring your top 3 projects with descriptions, tech stack, and links
- Your capstone deployed to Render or Railway — confirm `debug=False`, HTTPS enabled, and share the live URL
- A LinkedIn post announcing your completion: what you built, what was hardest, what you're building next — tag `#PythonIn100Days` and `@nikunjjakhotia`
- A self-assessment scorecard (from Day 100 exercises) rating your confidence in each major course topic
- An open-source pull request: find a beginner-friendly issue, fork the repo, and submit a meaningful contribution (docs fix, test, or small code change)
